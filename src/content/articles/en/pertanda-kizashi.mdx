# Fajar yang Ditunggu Bersama

## What Lies Beneath — Makna yang Tersembunyi

Pertanda (Kizashi) is not about dawn that has already arrived. It is about the hours before dawn, when the sky is still dark and the outcome is uncertain. The song centers on a group sitting together on a school rooftop. They carry sadness, a sense of futility, and the loss of earlier days, yet they choose to wait rather than go home.

The emotional conflict is simple and human: how to keep believing in tomorrow when only exhaustion remains. The song's answer is not loud optimism. It is togetherness and sensitivity to a "pertanda", a small sign that is enough to make someone move again. The narration speaks as "we", so hope here does not belong to one person. It is something lent, kept, and guarded together.

## Following the Feeling — Perjalanan Perasaan

### A School Rooftop and a Starless Sky

The song opens in a place that is both familiar and fragile: the rooftop of a school building, with cardboard to sit on and a sky without stars. There is no luxury and no guidance from above. There they talk about the future. The initial mood is calm but tense, like people who do not yet know whether dawn will bring an answer.

### Searching for Light Amid Futility

The first pre-chorus admits the dark side without decoration: deep sadness, days that are gone, and a feeling of futility because they "can't bear it all". But the sentence does not stop there. There is an active search for light for tomorrow. The feeling moves from weariness to seeking, and "hope" appears as something given by that light, not produced alone.

### A Roll Call That Becomes a Promise

The first chorus begins with a kind of roll call: you are here, I am here, he is here, she is here too. Naming people one by one feels like counting those who are still holding on. Then the eastern sky turns white, announcing a new morning. Togetherness is expressed through the body: arms around shoulders, moving forward. Tears and wounds are not denied. They are carried into the forward motion.

### A Waking City, a Time That Cannot Be Rewound

The second verse widens the view from the rooftop to a city opening its eyes and beginning to move. There is a longing for lost time, yet the sun is described as unchanged. It stays the same, and its warmth can be caught if a hand is reached out. This shift matters because loss cannot be undone, while warmth can still be reached now.

### From Fear to Trust

The second pre-chorus speaks directly to the listener. Do not fear invisible pain, because tears will dry in the end. The sign that was in the sky is now moved into the heart: "try to believe in the sign in your heart". The source of strength shifts from a phenomenon outside the self to a conviction within.

### From "Here" to "Never Alone"

The second chorus widens the circle from you and me to him and her, then turns "here" into gratitude: meeting each other, loving each other. The peak is the promise that wherever they are, no one is alone. The third chorus closes with the most concrete image: the sign will become a map for those who live on the road of dreams, and they will not give up. What began as a sign becomes a direction.

## Symbols & Metaphors — Simbolisme dan Makna Figuratif

- **The school rooftop**
  - Symbolic meaning: a threshold space between the life already lived and a future not yet clear.
  - Function in the song: the stage where the whole scene unfolds and where the future is discussed.
  - Link to the narrators' emotional experience: a familiar place makes anxiety feel shareable.
- **The starless sky and the whitening eastern sky**
  - Symbolic meaning: the darkness before hope, then dawn as the first sign that night will pass.
  - Function in the song: two states of the sky that frame the entire emotional journey.
  - Link to the narrators: the change of color gives a concrete reason to hold on.
- **The cardboard mat**
  - Symbolic meaning: simplicity and making do.
  - Function in the song: a small detail that makes the scene feel real and grounded.
  - Link to the narrators: hope does not wait for ideal conditions. It grows in humble places.
- **The sun and its warmth**
  - Symbolic meaning: something constant and dependable, whose warmth can be reached by extending a hand.
  - Function in the song: a contrast with lost time that cannot be returned.
  - Link to the narrators: it offers a source of comfort that is still available now.
- **The sign, from omen to map**
  - Symbolic meaning: a small cue that gives a reason to believe, and then grows into a guide and a direction.
  - Function in the song: the central idea, moving from the sky, to the heart, to the map of the final chorus.
  - Link to the narrators: reading signs is how they turn anxiety into steps.
- **Tears and invisible pain**
  - Symbolic meaning: inner wounds that cannot be seen but are real.
  - Function in the song: the counterweight to hope, acknowledged rather than hidden.
  - Link to the narrators: they are invited not to fear, because tears eventually dry.

## An Alternative Reading — Pembacaan Alternatif

The song can also be read as a reflection on parting and transition, for example the moment when a group that grew up together must move in different directions. Several details support this reading: the school setting, the talk about the future, the memory of days that are gone, and the promise that they are "anywhere" and "never alone". In this reading, dawn is not only a new day but the boundary between togetherness in one place and togetherness that holds across distance.

This reading is a possibility and is not stated directly by the lyrics. The song remains whole as a story of rising from sorrow together, without necessarily being about farewell.

## The Aftertone — Gema yang Tersisa

When the song ends, what remains is not a blinding light but the first white tint in the eastern sky, faint enough to doubt and clear enough to trust. The song suggests that hope rarely arrives as an answer. More often it arrives as a reason to keep sitting together until morning.

Perhaps what we call strength is only the courage to wait for dawn beside other people.
