# Jadi Matahari Buat Hujanmu

## What Lies Beneath — Makna yang Tersembunyi

From the title, *Bersama Kamu, Pelangi, dan Mentari (Kimi to Niji to Taiyou to)* sounds super bright. But it actually starts from a slightly cloudy moment: someone looking down, alone, and the "I" watching from a distance. That's where it gets interesting. The song isn't about **erasing sadness**, it's about **keeping someone company inside it**.

> A rainbow doesn't appear because the rain stops, but because a sun is willing to wait.

Notice one thing: the "I" never says "don't be sad". Instead they say **it's okay to cry**. They don't promise to stop the rain. Their promise is simpler and more honest: be the sun, then wait for the rainbow of your smile. So this is a song about **being there without pushing**.

## Following the Feeling — Perjalanan Perasaan

### 1. Watching from a Distance: You, Looking Down

The song opens with a rainbow in the post-rain sky, then the camera swings to someone on the left side of the school building, head bowed. The contrast hits right away: **the sky is already clearing, but this person isn't yet**. The "I" is in the seat of the one who sees, and that's where everything starts.

### 2. "It's Okay to Cry"

The key sentence is here: it's okay that you cry. Simple, but rarely heard in real life, since people usually go "okay, stop crying". In this song, tears get room to exist. Then there's a gentle promise that **a clear sky will arrive from afar**. Not right now, but it will come.

### 3. A Gap in the Clouds and Light That Pierces Through

The pre-chorus invites us to wait a moment in the gap of the clouds. Sadness will shine with light that pierces deep into the heart. It's a pretty idea: **sadness isn't light's enemy, it's where light gets in**. Light doesn't erase the dark, it passes through it.

### 4. Be the Sun, Not the Rain-Stopper

The chorus states the core wish: to protect in the circle of one's arms. But look at the shape of it. It's not "I'll fix everything", it's **I'll be the sun and wait for the rainbow of your smile**. A sun can't force a rainbow to appear. It can only be there, shining. Even the tears that fall are said to shine with seven colors of light.

### 5. Umbrella, Wind, and Kindness That Slowly Dries Things

The second verse moves to the practical: when hardship falls like heavy rain, the "I" will come with an umbrella for shelter. Then kindness is likened to wind that gently **dries the ground and the pole left wet after the rain**. Notice that an umbrella doesn't stop the rain, and wind doesn't dry everything at once. Every image here works **slowly**.

### 6. "All I Can Do Is..."

The second chorus shows a sweet humility. All the "I" can do is look for the rainbow and say how they feel, right after the rain lifts. It feels honest: they don't act like they can do everything. Then the ending says that even if love looks like it might vanish in a moment, **love will surely be seen when you want to cry**. Meaning, love shows up most clearly exactly when we need it most.

## Symbols & Metaphors — Simbolisme dan Makna Figuratif

- **The Rainbow (The Rainbow):** *Meaning:* hope and a smile that appear after a hard time. *Function:* the main image in the title and in nearly every part of the song. *Link to the "I":* they don't make the rainbow, they only look for it and wait for it to appear.
- **The Rain (The Rain):** *Meaning:* sadness and life's difficulties. *Function:* the backdrop that gives the rainbow meaning, since without rain there's no rainbow. *Link to the "I":* they don't reject the rain, they choose to accompany the person through it.
- **The Sun (The Sun):** *Meaning:* a warm, steady, patient presence. *Function:* the role the "I" picks in the chorus. *Link to the "I":* they want to be a source of light without forcing the sadness to end.
- **The Umbrella (The Umbrella):** *Meaning:* temporary shelter during a storm. *Function:* the concrete form of the promise to protect in the second verse. *Link to the "I":* they're ready to show up when needed, but they don't change the weather.
- **The Drying Wind (The Drying Wind):** *Meaning:* kindness that works slowly and almost unnoticed. *Function:* shows that healing is a process, not a sudden event. *Link to the "I":* they trust that small, steady kindness will let what's wet slowly dry.
- **Shining Tears (The Shining Tears):** *Meaning:* sadness given a new meaning, not thrown away. *Function:* connects crying to the seven-colored rainbow in the chorus. *Link to the "I":* they see those tears as something precious, not weakness.

## An Alternative Reading — Pembacaan Alternatif

The song can be read not as a romance but as **support between friends**. The school setting, the urge to protect, and the focus on warming kindness all fit a relationship of looking after each other. Read this way, "conveying my feelings" just means saying that you care and you're not alone.

There's also a more romantic reading: **a confession wrapped as comfort**. The line about conveying feelings to you can sound like a love declaration delivered in the gentlest way possible, by being present when someone is fragile. The lyrics don't confirm either one, so both make sense.

## The Aftertone — Gema yang Tersisa

When the song ends, one simple lesson sticks: **not everyone who is sad needs a solution, sometimes they just need company**. The rain will still fall, but the difference is that you're no longer facing it alone.

> **Not everyone needs to be cheered up with a fix; sometimes it's enough to have someone willing to be the sun while you wait for the rain to clear.**
