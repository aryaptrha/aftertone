# Hujan Belum Reda, Tapi Aku Sudah Bisa Tersenyum

## What Lies Beneath — Makna yang Tersembunyi

From the title, *After Rain* sounds like a song about **after** the rain, when the sky has cleared. But read slowly, the "I" in this song is actually **still in the middle of the rain**. The light is dimming, they feel broken, and they don't know whether their dream will be appreciated. What they do have is one small but real thing: **after crying, they can smile a little better than a moment ago**.

> Recovery rarely arrives all at once; usually it's just a little better than a moment ago.

That's what makes the song feel honest. They don't pretend to be fine, and they don't promise tomorrow will definitely be sunny. They just say: **I'll wait for the wind to change, and in the meantime I'll do what I can right now**. So this is a song about holding on through uncertainty, not about a guaranteed happy ending.

## Following the Feeling — Perjalanan Perasaan

### 1. Rain That Won't Stop

The song opens with a question that sounds really tired: how many more days will the rain keep falling? From there a resolve appears right away: they have to go out and look for the meaning of their life. Notice that **the rain and the big question about life show up together**. When days feel heavy, we often end up questioning the most basic things.

### 2. Looking Up and Asking the Trees

Though soaked through, the "I" is told to look up, then asks: what are the trees thinking? It's a funny and deep question at once. Trees don't run from the rain, they **stand where they are and keep growing**. The "I" seems to be looking for a role model among beings that know how to endure without saying much.

### 3. After Crying, a Little Lighter

The first chorus holds my favorite line: "compared to a moment ago, I can smile again". **Not "I'm completely fine now", but "better than before"**. This is a realistic kind of recovery. Then they turn to speak to "you": however heavy your day, from within the rain clouds there will surely be a colorful sky.

### 4. When the Light Starts to Dim

The second verse dips back into the fragile side. The light no longer reaches, and the "I" feels themselves growing dimmer. Then comes a very human fear: the dream they're fighting for, **will it be appreciated?** Here we see that the spirit of the chorus doesn't come from a heart that's always strong, but from one that can waver too.

### 5. Asking the Flowers, Growing Stronger Through the Rain

The second pre-chorus repeats the pattern, but this time it's the flowers being asked: what do they say about this sadness? In the second chorus, an answer seems to surface: after being beaten by rain, anyone can grow stronger. But once again they stay honest, because the very next line admits they're falling apart and **can only wait for the wind to change**, until just a little longer.

### 6. Waiting for the Wind to Change, While Still Moving

The final chorus combines two things that seem opposed: waiting and acting. They say they won't give up, then do what they can right now. **They can't control the direction of the wind, but they can control their own steps.** If the wind changes, the rain stops, the sky clears, tomorrow arrives. If not yet, they keep walking anyway.

## Symbols & Metaphors — Simbolisme dan Makna Figuratif

- **The Rain (The Rain):** *Meaning:* a hard stretch that feels endless. *Function:* the main backdrop covering almost the whole song. *Link to the "I":* they haven't left the rain yet, but they've found a way to face it.
- **The Colorful Sky Behind the Clouds (The Colorful Sky):** *Meaning:* the hope that good things still exist behind dark days. *Function:* the promise offered in the first chorus. *Link to the "I":* they hold onto that belief even though they haven't seen it themselves.
- **The Trees and the Flowers (The Trees and the Flowers):** *Meaning:* beings that endure and grow in the rain. *Function:* the ones the "I" turns to for questions and examples. *Link to the "I":* they learn that holding on doesn't have to mean talking a lot.
- **The Dimming Light (The Dimming Light):** *Meaning:* spirit and hope growing weaker. *Function:* an image of the "I"'s fragile side in the second verse. *Link to the "I":* they admit they're fading, yet still choose to keep going.
- **The Wind Direction (The Wind Direction):** *Meaning:* a change of circumstances outside our control. *Function:* the thing waited for in the second and third choruses. *Link to the "I":* they realize they can't force the wind, so they focus on what they can do.
- **The Cry (The Cry):** *Meaning:* a release that leaves the heart a bit lighter. *Function:* the turning point in the first chorus from heavy to able to smile again. *Link to the "I":* crying is treated as part of the process, not a sign of defeat.

## An Alternative Reading — Pembacaan Alternatif

The song can be read as **a story about chasing a dream**. The lines about searching for life's meaning, a fading light, and fear that a dream may go unappreciated sound like the experience of someone working hard without seeing results yet. In this reading, the rain is the stretch of hard work that nobody has acknowledged.

There's also a more personal reading: **the "I" is cheering themselves on**. In the first chorus they suddenly speak to "you", but that "you" could be their own self trying to believe. The lyrics don't say who is being addressed, so both readings are equally possible.

## The Aftertone — Gema yang Tersisa

When the song ends, what sticks isn't a promise of a clear sky but the honesty that **heavy days are heavy, and that's allowed**. You don't need to be fully healed to keep walking. A little better than a moment ago is enough, then do what you can right now.

> **It's okay not to be fully healed; being a little better than a moment ago is reason enough to keep going.**
