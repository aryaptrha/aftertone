# Naskah Cinta Pertama yang Ditulis Sendiri

## What Lies Beneath — Makna yang Tersembunyi

At first listen, *Bagai Cinta Pertama (Hatsukoi Ni Niteru)* sounds like a sweet, bouncy flirting song, complete with *fall in love*, *follow me*, and *ready go*. But look at the title: it says **"like"** a first love, not the first love itself. The "I" in this song never claims to be the first. They just ask, a little nervously: **could I feel like that to you?**

> The sweetest collision is the one you don't dodge on purpose.

What makes the song interesting is the gap between **super-confident style** and **a heart that's still unsure**. On one hand they say trust me, catch me, steal a head start. On the other they admit they're just an extra and keep asking "could it be". So it plays like someone **writing their own love script** with full flair, precisely because they're not sure they'll be loved back.

## Following the Feeling — Perjalanan Perasaan

### 1. A Pickup Line That Sounds Like an Order

The song opens with a rapid-fire list of invitations: trust that I'll make you happy, catch me, follow me, hug me, let's go anywhere together. It's fast and cheerful. But the line that wraps this part up is a question: could I be to you like a first love? **Behind all those "orders" is a very quiet plea.**

### 2. The Graduation Album and the Flat Days

Next comes a quiet scene. The person being addressed is flipping through a graduation album and sighing, the scenery feels boring, even the wind just turns pages. It's the mood of **the end of an era**: memories boxed up, days gone flat. This is where the "I" walks in, like color suddenly added to a black-and-white picture.

### 3. From Extra to Heroine

They admit even being an extra feels like too much to ask. Then, *bam*, a collision changes everything and suddenly they're the heroine. Love is described as a sudden impulse. The lyrics lean on story and stage language, as if **a role can switch in an instant because of one unplanned meeting**. The second chorus adds another layer: the other person's smile gets linked to "her" and to innocent days, as if they want to become something that feels like the most charming memory.

### 4. An Awkward Spring Walk

In the second verse, two people stroll a spring road with two swaying shadows. The distance feels odd, then slowly closes. You've probably felt a moment like this: **not holding hands yet, but no longer as far apart as strangers**. Awkward, but sweet.

### 5. Bubble Gum, Four Seasons, and "Don't Rush"

The mint flavor of freshly chewed bubble gum becomes a picture of love that wakes you up, no ordinary flavor needed. Then they make a plan: pedal hard uphill in summer, hold hands and dance in autumn, get to a kiss by winter. The funny part is they say "don't rush", right after urging "steal a head start". So **two opposite urges, wanting it fast and wanting it slow, live together in one heart**. The melting sunset and the orange color to cry over add the feeling that this youth is lovely and short.

### 6. Cupid, the Bud, and the "I Am Your Destiny" Claim

Heading toward the peak, Cupid finally finds the person, a hunch of love gets flustered, and a flower bud is ready to bloom. They find resolve from somewhere, then close with a bold claim: **I am your destiny**. It sounds super confident, but also like someone psyching themselves up right before the jump.

## Symbols & Metaphors — Simbolisme dan Makna Figuratif

- **The Graduation Album (The Graduation Album):** *Meaning:* past memories already boxed up, drawing a sigh. *Function:* the opening backdrop that sets a listless mood before love shows up. *Link to the "I":* they see that dullness as an opening to bring in something new.
- **The Collision and the Heroine Role (The Collision):** *Meaning:* an unplanned meeting that moves someone from the margin to the center of the story. *Function:* the song's turning point, where love is described as a sudden impulse. *Link to the "I":* they feel themselves change from spectator to lead.
- **The Mint Bubble Gum (The Mint Bubble Gum):** *Meaning:* a fresh, strong new sensation, the opposite of an ordinary taste. *Function:* a picture of love that keeps every sense awake. *Link to the "I":* they want a relationship that feels different from anything before.
- **The Four Seasons (The Four Seasons):** *Meaning:* a plan for togetherness that grows from light activities to a kiss. *Function:* lays out an imagined course for the future across a single year. *Link to the "I":* they dare to picture a long togetherness, even though reality isn't certain yet.
- **The Melting Sunset (The Melting Sunset):** *Meaning:* a beautiful moment you can't hold on to. *Function:* a gentle reminder not to rush and to enjoy the time you have. *Link to the "I":* amid all the eagerness, they realize this youth is short.
- **The Waiting Bud (The Waiting Bud):** *Meaning:* a feeling that's ripened and is waiting for the right moment to open. *Function:* the image right before the peak, tied to a heart that has borne fruit. *Link to the "I":* they get ready to declare themselves, though still nervous.

## An Alternative Reading — Pembacaan Alternatif

You can also read the song as **a romantic script being imagined**. The English invitations, the four-season plan, the heroine role, even Cupid, they all sound like scenes from a rom-com playing out in someone's head. Read this way, the confidence on the surface is **a rehearsal of courage**: they put together the best-case scenario so they won't be too scared of reality.

Another reading picks up on the words "idol song" and "like". The song can be seen as an attempt to be **someone who gives their listener that first-love feeling**, a presence that brings back the days of innocence. But "idol song" only appears once in the lyrics, so treat this as a possibility, not a replacement for the main reading about two people.

## The Aftertone — Gema yang Tersisa

When the song ends, what sticks isn't the loud destiny claim but the small question behind it: could I feel to you like a first love? The song reminds us that **the courage to fall in love often dresses up as confidence**, while the heart inside is still a bud waiting for warmth.

> **Some people find their first love; others dare to be the reason someone feels it.**
